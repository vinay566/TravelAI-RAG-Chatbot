
TRAVEL DESTINATIONS IN INDIA

1. Goa
Goa is a popular tourist destination known for its beaches, Portuguese heritage, churches, forts and cultural attractions.
Popular attractions include Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, Vagator Beach, Basilica of Bom Jesus and Fort Aguada.
Tourists can explore beaches, historical sites, museums and wildlife sanctuaries.

2. Kerala
Kerala is known for its backwaters, hill stations, beaches and natural landscapes.
Popular destinations include Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad and Kochi.
Visitors can experience houseboat journeys, tea plantations and local culture.

3. Rajasthan
Rajasthan is famous for its forts, palaces, deserts and historical heritage.
Popular destinations include Jaipur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer and Jodhpur.
Visitors can explore historical architecture, local markets and desert landscapes.

4. Himachal Pradesh
Himachal Pradesh is known for its mountains, valleys and hill stations.
Popular destinations include Manali, Shimla and Dharamshala.
Visitors can enjoy mountain scenery, trekking and nature tourism.

5. Telangana
Telangana offers historical monuments, cultural attractions and natural destinations.
Popular attractions include Charminar, Golconda Fort and Ramoji Film City in Hyderabad.
