
TRAVEL FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1. What is the best time to visit Goa?
The best time for many visitors is during the cooler and generally drier months from November to February. Check local weather before travelling.

Q2. What are popular places to visit in Kerala?
Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad and Kochi are popular destinations.

Q3. What should I consider before booking a hotel?
Check location, reviews, facilities, cancellation policies and total cost before booking.

Q4. How can I plan a budget trip?
Prepare a travel budget covering transportation, accommodation, food, sightseeing and emergency expenses.

Q5. What documents should I carry?
Carry valid identification, booking confirmations, tickets and any required permits or travel documents.

Q6. How can I plan a family trip?
Choose family-friendly destinations, suitable accommodation and convenient transportation. Prepare an itinerary with enough rest time.

Q7. Should I check the weather before travelling?
Yes. Check official weather forecasts and local advisories before departure.
