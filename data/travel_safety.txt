
TRAVEL SAFETY AND EMERGENCY GUIDE

1. Keep important documents safe.
Carry copies of identification, passport, tickets and travel insurance details.

2. Share your itinerary.
Inform family members or trusted contacts about your travel plans.

3. Protect personal belongings.
Keep money, identification and electronic devices secure.

4. Follow local rules.
Respect local laws, customs and safety instructions.

5. Medical emergencies.
Know the location of nearby hospitals and emergency services.

6. Lost passport during international travel.
Contact the nearest Indian Embassy or Consulate for assistance if you are an Indian passport holder.

7. Before international travel.
Check the latest travel advisories and passport and visa requirements for your destination.

8. Emergency preparation.
Keep emergency contact details and insurance information accessible.

Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India.
