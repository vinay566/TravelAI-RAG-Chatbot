
TRANSPORTATION GUIDE FOR INDIA

1. Railways
Indian Railways connects many cities and states.
Passengers can check train schedules, reservations and passenger information through official railway services.

2. Air Travel
Air travel is useful for covering long distances quickly.
Compare flight schedules, baggage allowances and ticket conditions before booking.

3. Road Transport
Buses, taxis and rental vehicles are common transportation options.
Check route conditions and vehicle safety before travelling.

4. Local Transport
Cities offer different local transportation options, including metro rail, buses, auto-rickshaws and taxis.

5. Railway Information
Check official railway information for train schedules, reservation details and passenger services.
